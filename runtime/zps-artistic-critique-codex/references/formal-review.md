# Formal acceptance and revision review

## Make findings actionable

For each consequential issue provide:

- Stable ID and priority: blocker for a demonstrated failure of a mandatory condition or essential purpose at this checkpoint; major for material quality/consistency; minor for polish. Label preferences separately.
- Artifact/region/state, inspection condition, evidence, likely consequence and confidence. Keep impact separate from certainty; label causal hypotheses and unverified risks.
- A supported repair direction, or a focused diagnostic check when competing causes require different repairs; what to preserve and how to verify the outcome.

Prioritize a few useful next actions. Acceptance concerns the required outcome, not obedience to the reviewer's technique; accept other solutions that satisfy it. Avoid vague fixes, invented metrics or unsupported exact pixel/color values. No supported issue is a valid result when evidence is sufficient.

A later-stage requirement is not automatically a blocker now. Missing evidence may hold a decision open without proving a defect. Scores are optional, require an explicit rubric, cannot conceal a blocker and must not compare unlike criteria. Mark missing evidence not assessable.

## Review revisions and close the loop

Identify changes to the brief, Bible or acceptance conditions before comparing versions. Separate unresolved defects, regressions and new requirements. Keep IDs; mark superseded/no longer applicable with a reason when requirements change, rather than counting this as a demonstrated repair or faulting a previously approved choice.

Resolve an issue only when the revised artifact demonstrates acceptance. Recheck affected areas, lost strengths and overall expression; missing views cannot prove resolution. Retain uncertainty where conditions or causes remain unclear.

Label self-review honestly. Claim independent review only with an actual separate reviewer context and blinding only for information withheld. Do not repeat reviews simply to obtain a preferred verdict.

Deliver strengths, prioritized findings/actions, evidence gaps and a verdict limited to the checks performed: meets checks, needs revision or insufficient evidence. A review recommendation is not owner approval or permission to ship.
