# ZPS - Visual Research - Codex

Select and synthesize references to answer a scoped art question. Research can precede direction selection or resolve questions within a chosen direction; preserve locked choices when they exist. It does not approve a finished style system.

## Frame the decision

Reuse the available brief and decision record. Identify the question, intended experience and relevant camera, scale, platform or scene conditions. Ask only about unknowns that would reverse the selection; otherwise state bounded assumptions and proceed.

Translate labels into questions: how silhouettes remain distinct, how values separate actors and terrain, or how material treatment supports mood. Cover the dimensions needed for this decision.

## Retrieve evidence

Search the project library first when relevant and accessible. Use its index and supplied files or storage tools. Library Manager can retrieve records and images; it need not produce a second artistic shortlist. If access is blocked, report the limit and continue with accessible evidence, without calling the library empty or fully searched.

Fill evidence gaps with external sources, preferring original creators/developers and credited collections. Captions, search snippets and tags are leads, not visual evidence. Retain source page/image locator and access date, or stable library/image IDs; distinguish credits from reuse permission.

Match evidence to the question: gameplay footage/screenshots for shown play conditions, concept sheets for construction, creator breakdowns for implementation. Official provenance alone does not make an image suitable for every claim. Generated exploration does not establish a shipped game's appearance. Label image type when known, otherwise unknown.

Work in rounds: retrieve, inspect, then narrow the search if needed. For consequential hypotheses, seek contrasting evidence within scope and budget; no counterexample quota is required. Stop when the question is answered or further search adds no useful distinction. Trend claims need dated evidence from multiple relevant examples.

## Inspect and curate

Open images through visual tools. Contact sheets support triage; inspect individual images for claims the sheet cannot support. Record overview/detail/inaccessible. Without visual access, retain source-based findings and candidate references but leave visual conclusions open; do not claim completed visual analysis.

Separate observations, interpretations and tests still needed. A still does not establish animation, player response or implementation; reduced-size viewing does not prove target-game readability. Record resolution, crop or transformation limits when consequential. Avoid invented measurements or confidence percentages.

Assess provenance, inspection depth and support for a claim separately. Uncertain authorship does not erase visible composition, but leaves attribution/reuse unresolved. Several images from a campaign or repost chain are not automatically independent corroboration.

Keep references that contribute a relevant principle or counterexample; remove redundant examples. Annotate their role: inspiration, execution reference with transfer limits, or an example to avoid for a specific brief conflict. One role does not make the image authoritative in every respect. Explain what to learn and what not to transfer; flag conflicting references instead of implying the style is already coherent.

## Synthesize and deliver

Own the artistic selection and synthesis; Library Manager owns retrieval and storage operations. Group evidence by question, distinguish shared mechanisms from particular treatments and keep important conclusions traceable to inspected images. A simple background, value separation and negative space may all reduce competition without establishing a universal dark-background rule.

For a proposed transfer, state the mechanism, observed conditions, relevant project differences and a test only where consequential uncertainty remains. Tested examples support their named scope, not universal applicability. Complete style-rule development belongs to Art Style Development.

Keep source/image identity, known credit/type, inspection scope, role, supporting observation and material transfer/rights limits in the research record. For a quick shortlist, present a link and concise reason per image, consolidating shared limitations; a formal research handoff needs fuller annotations. Reuse existing records rather than creating a separate file by default.

End with the supported recommendation and material gaps. Propose a next test only when it could change the decision; identify conditions and an observable outcome, without claiming it ran.

Saving or updating library records follows existing user authorization and project policy. Research alone does not authorize library mutation or bulk downloading. Delegate those responsibilities to Library Manager when needed; do not require another installed skill merely to complete a supported task.
